# Reminder template

Turn the requested cadence into one of `hourly`, `daily`, `weekly`, or `monthly`, with an explicit hour/minute and weekly or monthly day where required. Write a concrete task prompt, identify its chat, then call `schedule_task`. Ask for clarification when the time or requested outcome is ambiguous.
